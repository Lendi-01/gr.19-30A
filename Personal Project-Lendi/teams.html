🇬🇧 Premier League

Manchester United

Founded in 1878, Manchester United is one of England's most famous clubs. They have won many league titles and European trophies.

Liverpool

Founded in 1892, Liverpool is one of England's most successful clubs. They have won many league titles and Champions League trophies.

🇪🇸 La Liga

Real Madrid

Founded in 1902, Real Madrid is one of the most successful clubs in football history. They have won many Spanish and European trophies.

Barcelona

Founded in 1899, Barcelona is one of Spain's biggest clubs. They are famous for their attacking football and successful academy.

🇮🇹 Serie A

Juventus

Founded in 1897, Juventus is one of Italy's most successful clubs. They have won many Serie A titles.

AC Milan

Founded in 1899, AC Milan is one of Italy's most famous clubs and has a long history of success in Italy and Europe.
